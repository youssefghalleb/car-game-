Before You Start

  1. Join as a driver to create a car slot and receive a player ID.
  2. Scan the controller QR code on your phone and wait for Controller ready.
  3. Press Ready on the monitor. The host starts when every player is ready.

Driving Rules

  • Follow the track direction and pass checkpoints in order.
  • Wrong way, shortcuts, and missed checkpoints can invalidate your lap.
  • Ghost car appears only with one player in Practice or Time Trial.

Phone Steering Setup

  1. After pairing, tap Motion on the controller and allow sensor access.
  2. Open Sensors / motion, choose the axis that feels natural, then set steering to Stable if the car twitches.
  3. Use Start Calibration: hold the phone centered, save center, tilt left, save left, tilt right, save right.

        How to Play

        Use this as a quick reminder during setup or before a race.

        Lobby Flow

        1. Enter your name and choose race settings.
        2. Leave room empty to create one, or enter a code to join.
        3. Click Join Game, then pair your phone controller.
        4. Press Ready. The host starts when everyone is ready.

        Phone Controller

        • Scan the QR code shown after joining.
        • Confirm room and player are auto-filled.
        • Tap Connect and wait for Controller paired.
        • Use throttle, brake, nitro, and tilt or touch steering.

        Sensor Calibration

        1. Tap Motion on the controller and accept phone sensor permission.
        2. Open Sensors / motion if steering feels inverted, twitchy, or off-center.
        3. Use Stable steering for Track 2 or first-time testers.
        4. Run Start Calibration: center phone, save center, tilt left, save left, tilt right, save right.
        5. If steering is reversed, toggle Invert and test again before pressing Ready.

        Keyboard Backup

        • W or Up Arrow accelerates.
        • S or Down Arrow brakes or reverses.
        • A/D or Left/Right Arrow steer.
        • Space uses nitro.

        Race Modes

        • Sprint: complete the selected laps first.
        • Time Trial: solo lap-time challenge.
        • Practice: free driving for learning the track.
        • Elimination: avoid being the slowest car.

        Rules

        • Pass checkpoints in order.
        • Avoid wrong-way driving and shortcuts.
        • Off-track driving slows you down and can trigger warnings.
        • Track 2 has stricter anti-cheat checks.

        Spectator / Ghost

        • Join as Spectator to watch without creating a car.
        • Spectators do not need a phone controller.
        • Ghost car works only with one player in Practice or Time Trial.
